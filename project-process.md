**Evidence limit:** three pages on one evening show how competitors present themselves; they do not prove what gym-goers want or that a quiet brand will sell.

### Positioning (two options)

| Option | Who it serves | Why they would choose it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — Quiet dedication | An experienced lifter who trains most days and doesn't want a loud, logo-heavy shirt | A calm shirt that matches how they already train: show up, do the work, no hype |
| B — A steady start | A beginner building the gym habit who finds big gym brands intimidating | An encouraging, low-pressure reminder to keep showing up, without the "jacked" image |

### Persona hypotheses (two)

| Field | Persona 1 — experienced lifter | Persona 2 — gym beginner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Situation | Heading to the gym before work, 5+ days a week | A few weeks into going to the gym; still unsure of routines |
| Goal | Let consistent effort speak; plain gear that holds up | Motivation to keep showing up |
| Uncertainty | Fit for a muscular build; whether it's "just another gym tee" | Feeling out of place in gym-brand culture; wasting money |
| Evidence | My own picture of bodybuilding (Brand Studio, Nov 3) + competitor pages above | AI-proposed in Brand Studio; I added "motivation" |
| Assumption | That some lifters prefer quiet over hype — **not checked with any lifter** | That beginners feel intimidated by loud brands — **not checked** |

### Primary audience

**Choice: Persona 1 / Option A (experienced lifter).** For a lifter who trains consistently, Quiet Reps offers one plain white tee that matches quiet dedication. We emphasize a calm voice and a clear single price instead of hype, discounts and urgency. We still need to check whether lifters actually want a quiet shirt, and whether fit guidance for a muscular build matters most. Persona 2 stays as the check: the same page should not feel cold to a beginner.

### Look, brand character and honest persuasion (reused from SPEC.md)

- **Look:** postmodernist layered shirt treatment (Weingart reference), soft sage and lavender, Georgia headings — a calm contrast to the black/white, promo-banner competitors.
- **Character:** Hero, voice = calm, determined, encouraging. Example line: "Show up. Put in the reps."
- **Reciprocity:** free pre-session ritual (pause, name one reason to show up) — useful before any purchase. Usefulness is a hypothesis.
- **Commitment & consistency:** optional training intention ("Stay consistent") that never adds anything to the bag.
- **What I won't do:** no invented reviews or review counts, no "Last Chance" or countdowns without a real limit, no unsupported fabric/fit claims, no stacked fake discounts.
